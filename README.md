Welcome to an extraordinarily grueling, deeply meticulous, and relentlessly calculated survival experience designed exclusively for engineers who find standard automation far too forgiving. This hyperrealistic modpack strips away every traditional convenience, forcing you to meticulously manage every single ounce of mechanical stress, thermodynamic efficiency, and biological containment just to survive a hostile, mutating environment. Every single action demands precise logistic planning, turning simple resource gathering into an exercise in industrial and environmental management.

The foundational backbone of your survival relies on the intricate mechanics introduced by Create, its heavy diesel-industrial expansion The Factory Must Grow (TFMG), and the revolutionary aerial framework of Create: Aeronautics. Rather than casually placing blocks, you must calculate precise rotational stress units (SU), transfer kinetic energy through complex gearshifts and clutches, and eventually transition from basic water wheels to massive coal-coke ovens, steel-casting blast furnaces, and pressurized oil distillation towers. Establishing a stable supply chain requires laying down exact pipe networks for liquid fuel, configuring multi-block assembly lines with mechanical crafters, and engineering fully functional, physics-compliant airships and mobile multi-block factories complete with localized control panels, combustion engines, and structural stress tolerances.

Complementing this heavy engineering layer is the biological pressure cooker driven by Spore, which fundamentally transforms the world into a living, adapting nightmare. You cannot simply build a sprawling factory and leave it unattended; environmental pollution and biological encroachment require constant defensive infrastructure, advanced filtration systems, and hermetically sealed perimeters. Every byproduct from your diesel engines and industrial refineries risks accelerating ecological retaliation, forcing you to balance high-yield mass production with rigorous hazard containment, automated turret defense grids, and deeply calculated resource loops to ensure your survival against an encroaching, relentless evolutionary threat.
